File separate fixes

Shared parser: month-first interpretation

For day-first tenants, the shared parser reads ambiguous dates month-first. R-41200 reports an intended 7 April appearing as 4 July; R-41201 reports the reverse. The expected behavior is to retain the intended date according to an explicit input contract.

See the parser issue draft. Verify the affected input paths across tenant locales, with month-first inputs as controls. The export documents reported symptoms and classification; it does not include a production parser to reproduce live.

Booking-v3 form: hint and parser disagree

The form prints MM/DD/YYYY for day-first tenants while reading their input in tenant order. A person following that hint can therefore get a swapped date even when tenant-aware parsing works as designed. The affected tenant configurations are alder.physio, copper.studio and pale.harbour. R-41230, R-41241 and R-41251 illustrate this path.

See the form-hint issue draft. Align the displayed hint and accepted format, then verify booking-v3 separately from the shared parser. Include booking-v2 and month-first locales as controls.

Keep the timing anomalies visible

The desk’s notes flag R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344 and R-41620. Their bodies describe one-day differences, an invite apparently preceding a booking, or different days across locations. They remain counted in the parser group under the supplied tenant-based classifier.

That filing rule does not prove their underlying cause. Preserve those report IDs for follow-up; do not double-count them, subtract them silently, or call them a confirmed timezone defect. Check original timestamps, timezone handling and reproduction evidence before deciding whether another ticket is warranted.

The queue did not begin with the merge

Reports arrived before the large July merge and continued afterward. A spike in merges describes desk activity, not necessarily a new product incident. The fact that an issue is labelled known does not establish that it has been fixed or that reporting has stopped.
